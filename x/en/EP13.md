# Episode 13. One Night Is Enough

Saturday, 7:40 a.m. The main conference room on the 29th floor.

The last slide came up on the screen.

'A joint proposal from the Daily Beauty Division and the Online Strategy Team - a subscription for Slowash family-size body wash.'

Under it, in small type, were two more lines.

'Prepared by: Manager Lee Woojin and Na Seohyun, Online Planning Part.'

Director Cho Youngseok finished his presentation and paused on that slide. Then he raised a hand toward the extra seats by the door.

"To be honest, those two stayed up all night to build this. Our division supplied the product."

A few heads in the room turned. Woojin straightened his back. Beside him, Seohyun gripped the notebook on her lap with both hands. It was her first time in the Saturday meeting. She had been let in to carry the printouts and to turn every water bottle so the label faced the right way.

President Moon Hyekyung looked at the last slide for a long time.

"Nice. A joint proposal."

The red pen didn't move. Instead she flipped the printout back to the draft of the product page.

"But this page. Does a customer actually read all of this?"

The draft was packed with text. Twelve ingredient names, five certification badges, and 'skin barrier,' 'deep moisture' and 'low irritation' three times each. Every sentence had been lifted straight from the Daily Beauty Division's in-store display guide.

Cho hesitated. Han Jiseok spoke up instead.

"We'll redo the product page by next week. About two weeks..."

"Two weeks?"

That was when it happened. A very small voice slipped out from the seats by the door.

"SODA LAB does this in one night."

The room went quiet.

Seohyun looked like she wanted to clap a hand over her own mouth. She had been talking to herself. Truly, to herself. But that was not a room where talking to yourself stayed that way.

Moon turned her head.

"Who was that?"

Woojin stopped breathing. Seohyun slowly stood up.

"Na Seohyun, Online Planning Part. I'm sorry."

"Sorry for what? Say it again. SODA LAB does this in one night?"

Seohyun looked at Jiseok. He gave the smallest nod. It meant she could say it. No, it meant she had already said it, so she might as well finish.

"Yes. When SODA LAB launches something, the marketer writes the page herself the night before and it goes up the next morning. If something's wrong, they fix it that day. They watch how customers react and change the wording again in the afternoon. We have to get it reviewed by legal, the brand team, the division and design."

"Hence two weeks."

"Yes."

"And a page written in one night sells better?"

Seohyun hesitated for a moment. Then she took out her phone.

"A friend from college is a marketer at SODA LAB. Jung Daeun. Last month she wrote a moisturizer page in one night, and this was the first line."

She read from the screen.

"'Put it on within three minutes of washing your face. That's all.'"

Someone in the room gave a small laugh. It was VP Oh Jeongtaek, the head of finance.

"That page converts twice as well as our Sugyeol cream page. She sent it to show off, so whether that's accurate... I'm not sure."

Her voice got smaller on the last words. Moon didn't miss them.

"You say you're not sure when you're not sure. Good."

She picked up the red pen and wrote something in the margin of the printout.

"Senior Manager Han. Not two weeks. One week. Next Saturday, bring me two new pages - our Sugyeol cream and that body wash. VP Kwon, you'll sort out the review process with the CEO."

CEO Hwang Inseok nodded. Kwon Jaehyuk only said a short "Yes."

8:12 a.m. The meeting was over.

In the emptied room, Seohyun stood holding on to the back of a chair. Her knees were shaking.

"Sir. I'm getting fired now, right?"

Woojin held back a laugh.

"The President said 'good.'"

"Is that good?"

"In three weeks, today's the first time she's said 'good' to anyone."

Only then did Seohyun sink into the chair. Then she looked up.

"But sir. If it's one week, what about the reviews?"

Woojin didn't know that either.

9 a.m. VP Kwon's office.

Kwon was standing at the window, looking down toward Gwanghwamun. When Jiseok, Baek Seungwon and Woojin came in, he turned around.

"How many review steps are there right now?"

"Five, sir. The division, the brand team, legal, design, and then the report to the CEO's office."

Jiseok answered.

"Which of those steps is for the customer?"

No one answered. Kwon wrote the five on the whiteboard and drew a line through three of them.

"Legal stays. Ad copy is genuinely risky. The division only checks facts. Everything else gets reported after the fact. I'll tell the CEO myself."

"The brand team will push back."

Baek said it.

"Let them. But put it in writing that if we get it wrong, we take responsibility. Seungwon, you calm the brand team down for me."

"Yes, sir."

Baek smiled. It was the first time 'Seungwon' had come out in that office. Woojin pretended he hadn't heard and kept his eyes on his notebook. Kwon seemed to realize what he had said too, and cleared his throat once.

"And Han."

"Yes."

"The new hire from today. Na Seohyun."

Jiseok tensed. Kwon added,

"Let her write the first line of the page. Don't you fix it."

Saturday, 2 p.m. 「Midday Library」, a café in Seongsu.

Seohyun waited for Jung Daeun at a window seat. They had been in the same beauty club in college. After graduation Daeun joined SODA LAB when it had twelve employees, and Seohyun joined Cheongyeon, which had thousands. Three years later, SODA LAB had more than two hundred.

Daeun came in with a laptop under her arm. Even on a Saturday, she had a working face on.

"I heard you talked about me in front of your president today? There are people at my company who know my friend works at Cheongyeon. Word travels fast."

"Sorry. I really was talking to myself."

"Who talks to herself in front of the president?"

Daeun laughed and ordered a coffee. Then she came back with a serious face.

"But Seohyun. Writing it in one night isn't something to brag about."

§IMG3§

"What?"

"We write it in one night because there's nobody to review it. If it's wrong, it's on me. Last month I got one ingredient name wrong and answered three hundred customer questions over three days. By myself."

Seohyun put down her cup.

"Your company has five places checking everything. It's slow, sure. But those five places also protect you. Nobody protects us."

Daeun looked out the window. In the Seongsu alley, there was a line in front of SODA LAB's pop-up store.

"Still, I like my company. I can see the sentence I wrote selling the next day. Have you ever seen a sentence you wrote sell?"

Seohyun couldn't answer. For six months, every sentence she had written had gone out under someone else's name.

That night, 11 p.m. Seohyun sat at the desk in her studio apartment and opened a blank document.

Sugyeol moisturizing cream. Her part leader, who came from the lab, said he knew the formula. A senior on the data side pulled the customer reviews for her. Her manager sent twenty screenshots of competitors' pages.

She wrote the first line. Deleted it. Wrote it again.

2 a.m. One line was left at the top of the document.

§IMG4§

'Put it on in the morning. Forget it until night.'

Seohyun stared at that line for a long time. And for the first time, she didn't wonder whose name it would go out under.
