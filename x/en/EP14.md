# Episode 14. The Chemistry of a Product Page

Monday, 8 a.m. A worn-out notebook lay on Han Jiseok's desk.

The corners of its brown cover were rubbed away. On the cover, in ballpoint pen, was written 'Formulation Notes 03 - 2008.' Woojin saw it as he set down his coffee.

"Sir, what's that?"

"My notebook from the lab."

§IMG1§

Jiseok opened it. The pages were crowded with chemical formulas and numbers. Between them were small pencil drawings. Cream spreading thin over skin, arrows of moisture escaping.

"When they first worked out the formula for Sugyeol moisturizing cream, I was the one washing the beakers. My second year as a researcher."

"You made this cream, sir?"

"Someone else made it. I recorded every version she threw away."

Jiseok's hand stopped on one page. 'Version 17 - separated. Three days in summer heat.' Below it was written 'Version 23 - passed.' It was circled three times.

"This cream passed on the twenty-third try. But the product page says none of that. 'Deep moisture,' 'skin barrier,' 'low irritation.' The same three phrases as every other company's cream."

Na Seohyun came over with her laptop. On her screen was the line she had written at dawn on Saturday.

'Put it on in the morning. Forget it until night.'

Jiseok read it out loud. Then he said nothing for a long time. Seohyun got nervous first.

"It's too light, isn't it? I'll fix it."

"No. Don't fix it."

Jiseok turned the notebook toward her.

"But for this line not to be a lie, there has to be proof under it. Proof that cream you put on in the morning is still there at night."

"Is there data like that?"

"There is. From 2008. The lab measured skin moisture for twelve hours. I'm the only one who knows where that graph is."

Seohyun's eyes went wide.

10 a.m. A video call with the research lab.

The woman on the screen was in a white coat. Group Leader So Eunjeong, Formulation Development Group, Cheongyeon Basic Research Lab. Fifty-one. She was the one who first worked out the formula for Sugyeol moisturizing cream.

"Han Jiseok? You're still at the company?"

"I'm still here."

"When you left the lab for marketing, you said you'd be back in three years."

"It's been eleven."

So laughed. Then she looked at Seohyun, sitting beside him.

"So, what do you need?"

"The moisture retention data for Version 23. The clinical results from summer 2008."

"I can't release that. To use it in advertising, it has to be verified again."

"I know. I'm not going to use the numbers. I just want to describe the shape of the graph. So customers can understand it."

So thought for a moment. Then she spoke to someone off screen.

"Mijin, can you find the 2008 Sugyeol folder?"

§IMG2§

A young researcher leaned into the edge of the frame. Researcher Nam Mijin of the Formulation Development Group. Second year at the company.

"Ma'am, that's in the basement archive. On paper."

"Then go down."

Mijin sighed and disappeared. So looked at Jiseok.

"Jiseok. Do you remember washing those beakers?"

"Yes."

"You said something back then. That this cream was good, but people would never know why. It took thirteen years."

Tuesday, 3 p.m. The Online Planning Part.

The scan arrived from the lab. One graph on yellowed paper. Time on the horizontal axis, skin moisture on the vertical. The lines for the other creams dropped to the bottom after six hours. The line for Version 23 was still above half its starting height at twelve hours.

"This is it."

Seohyun said it. Jiseok nodded, but he didn't look happy.

"But legal won't let us use this graph. It's from 2008."

"Then what if we say it in words instead?"

§IMG3§

Seohyun drew on the whiteboard. Two cups of water. One had a big hole, one had a small hole.

"This isn't a cream that puts in more water. It's a cream that makes the water leave more slowly. Right?"

Jiseok looked at the drawing and laughed. What he hadn't been able to explain in thirteen years, a twenty-six-year-old new hire had drawn with two cups.

"Right. Exactly."

"Then let's write it like this. 'Instead of adding more water, we made it leave less.'"

Woojin wrote it down beside them. Choi Yoona leaned out from behind her monitor.

"Looking at the review data, the word people who bought Sugyeol cream used most is 'afternoon.' 'Still not tight in the afternoon,' 'moist into the afternoon.' Three hundred out of twelve hundred reviews."

"Put that on the second line of the page."

Jiseok said it.

"It's the customers' own words. Legal won't have anything to say."

Wednesday, 7 p.m. The legal team's meeting room.

Senior Manager Gil Hyunsu of the legal team turned the pages of the printout, picking up his red pen and putting it down again. Forty-four. Twelve years of reviewing ad copy. He had struck out thousands of sentences.

"'Forget it until night.' That could look like an exaggerated claim."

"'Forget it' isn't a claim. It's a suggestion."

Jiseok said it. Gil pushed his glasses up.

"Then what about 'we made it leave less'? Where's the evidence?"

"There's internal data from the lab. We'll have it re-verified for public use by next month. Until then we'll phrase it as 'what the formula was designed to do.'"

Gil looked down at the printout for a long time. Then he put down the red pen.

§IMG4§

"Senior Manager Han. In twelve years, this is the first time I've seen marketing bring the evidence first."

"I came from the lab."

"No. Usually they write the copy first and ask us to go find the evidence."

He made a small check mark next to 'forget it.' That meant it passed.

Saturday, 7:50 a.m. The main conference room.

The new product page came up on the screen. The first line was Seohyun's. The second line was a customer review. On the third line was the drawing of two cups.

President Moon Hyekyung took out her phone and opened the page herself. She scrolled down and stopped.

"This drawing of the cups. Who drew it?"

"Associate Na Seohyun."

Jiseok answered. Moon nodded.

"And this line? 'Instead of adding more water, we made it leave less.'"

Jiseok hesitated for a moment. They had made that line together. Half of it was the lab's thirteen years, and half was a new hire's drawing of two cups.

"It's Group Leader So Eunjeong's formula from the lab, put into words by Associate Na Seohyun."

Moon wrote something with her red pen.

"That's the first time someone from the lab has been named in this meeting."

After the meeting, Jiseok took out his phone and sent a message to the lab.

[Sunbae, your name came up in the President's meeting today. Thanks to Version 23.]

The reply came ten minutes later.

[My name got called after thirteen years. Mijin says she breathed archive dust for this, so you're buying her dinner.]

The following Monday. Yoona put up the weekly report.

Conversion rate for the Sugyeol moisturizing cream product page. 1.7 times the week before.

The whole part cheered quietly. Only Yoona didn't smile. Next to the number she added a note in small letters.

'Caution - a coupon event ran the same week. Need to separate the page effect from the coupon effect.'
