# Episode 10. The Saturday Drop-off

Friday, 11:04 p.m. Lee Woojin's phone buzzed.

[Woojin, I'm sorry. I don't think I can present tomorrow. Seoa's fever is up to 39 degrees. My husband's away on a site trip.]

§IMG1§

It was Choi Yoona. Her husband, Choi Jeonghun, was a site manager at a construction company. His apartment project in Sejong was in its final stretch, so for a month he had been living down there during the week, and this week the completion inspection meant he couldn't come home even for the weekend. This Saturday, at the third weekly report, Yoona was supposed to present the results. Three days since they had cut the checkout from nine steps to five. The slot where someone had to say, in numbers, what those three days had changed. And the person who had built those numbers was her.

[Don't worry about it. Stay with Seoa.]

[Everything's in the shared folder. My presenter notes too. But who's going to do it?]

Woojin started to type and stopped. Han Jiseok had to present the nine-step analysis. Baek Seungwon had the business-division cooperation update. Kwon Jaehyuk had the overview. That left one person.

Jiseok's name popped up on the screen. The team chat.

[Han Jiseok] Woojin takes Yoona's results section.
[Lee Woojin] Me?
[Han Jiseok] You've looked at those numbers more than anyone. You pulled the 22-hour coupon data too.
[Baek Seungwon] You can do it, Woojin. The President doesn't eat people.
[Kwon Jaehyuk] Results section, three minutes. Just get the first sentence exactly right.

Just get the first sentence exactly right. Woojin held on to that line all night.

1:12 a.m. The phone buzzed again. Not the team chat this time - a direct message. When Woojin saw who it was from, he sat up in bed.

[Kwon Jaehyuk] Memorize the first sentence and go to sleep.
[Kwon Jaehyuk] Manager Choi's notes will do the rest.
[Kwon Jaehyuk] And wear only one tie.

He wouldn't understand the last line until the next morning. Woojin typed "Yes," deleted it, typed "Thank you," deleted that too, and finally sent "Yes, sir." The little "1" beside the message vanished at once. The VP was still awake too.

3 a.m. He sat at the desk in his one-room studio and read Yoona's presenter notes out loud. They were clean. *Checkout steps 9 → 5. Change in checkout drop-off over the 3 days since. Share of guest one-click purchases. Effect of removing the "Yeonhwi excluded" pop-up.* Beside every number, in her small, neat type, there was a note. *If the President asks: admit first that 3 days is short.*

He rewrote the first sentence twelve times. He wrote the thirteenth at the top of his notebook.

*Until last week, customers tapped nine times. Starting this week, they tap five.*

At the same hour, Yoona was sitting on the floor of her daughter's room.

Seoa had taken a fever reducer and finally fallen asleep. The cooling patch on her forehead was half peeled off. Yoona pressed it back down and took the child's hand. It was small and hot.

On her phone, the presenter notes were open. She had been editing them for three hours. To make them easier for Woojin to read, she kept adding notes, one at a time. *Take a breath here.* *Say this number slowly.*

4:30 a.m. Seoa opened her eyes.

"Mommy, is it Saturday today?"

"Yes."

"Then are you going to work again?"

Yoona couldn't answer. For the last two Saturdays, her daughter had woken up without her mom. She had eaten the rice balls her dad made, picked up the lunchbox her mom had packed the night before, and gone to the Saturday childcare class.

"No. Today Mommy's staying home. All day."

"Really?"

"Really."

Seoa squeezed her mother's hand and fell back asleep. Without letting go, Yoona used her other hand to leave a message in the team chat.

[Woojin, I've uploaded the notes again. I'm really sorry. Tomorrow too... no, today too. Sorry.]

5:10 a.m. When Woojin tapped his badge at the security gate on the first floor of Cheongyeon Tower, the man at the security desk looked up.

"Saturday again, huh, Manager Lee?"

Tak Mingyu, night shift, security. The one who had called on that first Saturday morning because the lights on the 29th floor wouldn't go off. Forty-one. Six years on the Cheongyeon Tower security team and nowhere else. He said he knew every face on the 29th floor by now.

§IMG2§

"How many of you today?"

"Sixteen."

"Then I'll hold one elevator on 29. Before the President gets here."

Tak said it and went back to his monitors. Nobody had asked him to. In three weeks, Woojin had started to understand that it was small things like this, piled up, that kept a meeting running.

5:30 a.m., the 29th floor. Woojin switched on the lights in the main conference room. The third Saturday. Sixteen bottles of room-temperature water, sixteen glasses, black and red pens. He no longer needed Yang Eunju to tell him. His hands remembered.

Pages 9 to 11 of the sixteen printed copies were his. He flipped through those three pages sixteen times, checking the angle of each staple. His hands were shaking.

"Nervous?"

Baek Seungwon walked in carrying kimbap instead of cup noodles. Last week, ramyeon. This week, kimbap. You could read the tension of the meeting from what he brought. Seaweed rolls packed tight with rice, egg, pickled radish and spinach - the kind of food you have to chew slowly, one round slice at a time.

"Yes."

"The first time I reported to the President, I wore two ties."

"Two?"

"I was so nervous at dawn I forgot I already had one on, and I put on another. I only noticed in the meeting room. And nobody said a word. Afterward the President told me, 'Senior Manager Baek, one tie is enough.'"

Woojin laughed. For the first time, his shoulders loosened a little.

6:20. Yang Eunju came up first. She walked once around the room, checking that every water bottle label faced the same way. Then she looked at Woojin's printout and asked,

"Are you presenting the results today, Manager Lee?"

"Yes. Manager Choi's daughter is sick."

Eunju looked at him for a moment. The eyes of someone who had worked for Moon Hyekyung for ten years. Eyes that knew what made the President pick up her pen, and what made her put it down.

"The President only listens to the first sentence. The rest she reads off the page. So the first sentence just has to be a number."

She said only that and went back toward the President's office. Woojin looked at the first sentence in his notebook again. Nine times. Five times. Numbers.

6:58. The room went quiet. 7:00 sharp. Moon Hyekyung came in and sat down.

Kwon Jaehyuk's overview, Han Jiseok's nine-step analysis, Baek Seungwon's cooperation update. Twenty-five minutes passed. Hyekyung never once picked up the red pen. Instead she nodded a few times. A nod was something that hadn't happened on the last two Saturdays.

"Next are the results. Manager Lee Woojin will report in place of Manager Choi Yoona."

Jaehyuk said it.

Woojin stood. Sixteen pairs of eyes turned to him. He looked down at his notebook. The first sentence was there. The sentence he had rewritten thirteen times.

He opened his mouth.

§IMG3§

"Until last week..."

His voice didn't come.

He couldn't tell where the next word had gone. The writing in the notebook blurred in front of him. The air in the room grew heavy. Someone shifted in a chair. CEO Hwang Inseok glanced at his watch. VP Eom Kitae folded his arms.

One second.

Two seconds.

Three seconds.

He remembered the few seconds on the first meeting day when Jaehyuk had searched for words in front of the President's "Why?" Now that silence was his.

Hyekyung put down her pen.

"Manager Lee."

Woojin looked up.

"Take your time. It's fine even if the number is wrong. Just say it."

Her voice wasn't loud. It was lower than any voice he had heard in this room on the last two Saturdays.

Woojin took one breath. Yoona's note came back to him. *Take a breath here.*

"Until last week, customers tapped nine times."

His voice came back.

"Starting this week, they tap five."

Hyekyung turned to page 9. She looked at one graph for a long time. Woojin said the next sentence. And the one after that. One by one, the notes Yoona had left at dawn held him up.

At the same hour, Yoona was lying next to Seoa, watching her phone screen.

Messages from Baek Seungwon were coming into the team chat one at a time. He seemed to be typing under the conference table. Lots of typos.

[Baek Seungwon] woojin starting
[Baek Seungwon] stuck on first sentnce
[Baek Seungwon] president said take your time

Yoona stopped breathing. For the full minute before the next message came, she couldn't do anything.

[Baek Seungwon] started again. doing well
[Baek Seungwon] doing yoonas notes exactly. took a breath

Yoona held the phone to her chest and closed her eyes. Beside her, Seoa stirred and wrapped her arms around her mother's arm. The child's forehead was a little less hot than it had been at dawn.

§IMG4§

When three minutes had passed, Hyekyung looked up.

"Who made these numbers?"

"Manager Choi Yoona. Her daughter has a fever today, so she's at home."

The room went quiet again. This silence was different from the one before. Woojin couldn't read Hyekyung's face.

She picked up the red pen again.

(End of Episode 10)
